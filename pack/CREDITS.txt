The 10 look resolvers under assets/cobblemon/bedrock/pokemon/resolvers and the files under assets/cobblemon_origins_tweaks (look recipes, recipe masks, eye layers, the Primal Groudon geometry patch) are copies of the Cobblemon Origins Tweaks 1.3.1 files for looks on Mega and Primal forms (Illusionary Rayquaza, Shadow Mewtwo X/Y, Armored Mewtwo, Shadow Latias, Latios and Heatran, Clone Venusaur, Charizard X/Y and Blastoise, Meta Groudon), licensed MIT. None of them is derived from Cobblemon's CC BY-NC assets: the masks only mark which pixels to recolor, and the eye layers and geometry patch are original work. The colors are applied on the player's computer to the Mega Showdown and zamega textures they have installed. No Mega Showdown, zamega or Cobblemon art files are included. Remove these files once every client runs Tweaks 1.3.1 or newer.
